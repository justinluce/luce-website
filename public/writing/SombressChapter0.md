*~1,000 years ago*

Drifting through space should be eternal serenity. Floating on nothing, wrapped among the stars. Suspended in time, yet remaining far too long. You close your eyes, savoring the tranquility.

It doesn’t last.

A sound punctures your peace. Curious. Should sound be in space? You can’t remember. But listen — it’s a voice! A voice so distant you would think it comes from even further beyond. It echoes from everywhere all at once, the words of someone from a past life. It lets you know that you are going home.

Somewhere nearby you feel a warmth, something you haven’t felt for eons. In the distance is a misty cloud emanating heat that slinks out to wrap around your torso. It’s a radiant pull so strong and so incessant that you can’t ignore its call. The voice grows louder, soothing, yet more compelling. Irresistible. You follow, a celestial pilgrim swimming in the expanse, growing closer to the sensation. You stare into the mist, and your eye catches brief images of what it must contain. Squinting, you see a small home glowing in the sunset at the foot of a huge ice-capped mountain, with calm seas stretching just beyond. It’s a place so different, so much better, from the life you’re living. Tears bubble up and linger in your eyelids. Why? You don’t know, but you can’t stop crying. You swim faster, your arms flailing to reach what should be yours. Faster, faster — closer, closer. Suddenly it’s so close, it taunts you from just outside your grasp. If you could breathe you would be whimpering, crying out for salvation. Please! You deserve another chance! You promise it’s the only thing you’ll ever desire again! You reach out, finally brushing it with your fingertips!

It’s gone.

The warmth has faded, replaced only by a grim chill.

You are alone. Drifting through space, lost and forgotten again.

It was so close, but you were not enough.
